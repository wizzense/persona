Settings

Microphone

Which device captures your voice, and whether it's muted.

Takes effect on your next talk (no restart needed). If the saved device disappears, the system default is used instead.

Talk mode

How the mic starts and stops listening.

Click the avatar to toggle talking. Press and hold it (~a quarter second) to talk while held, release to stop — this works today regardless of the mode above, which only affects the keyboard shortcut.

Hotkeys

Click Rebind, then press the key combo you want. Conflicts are checked before saving.

Updates

Aither World bricks installed on this machine. Upgrade tests the brick afterwards and rolls back on its own if the test fails.
