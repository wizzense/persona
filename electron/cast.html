Advanced · What this desk will show

Mature characters need an adult opt-in and a verified age. Turning them on asks the platform — only it can attest the age half — and turning them off always works, even with the fleet down. Under that, this desk has its own ceiling, and a character nobody has rated stays hidden with the mature ones until you rate the roster.

Advanced · Physics for everyone

How much every body moves. Each fader multiplies what the model's author set (×1 = as authored). A body's own Physics under Bodies overrides these for that body only.

Advanced · Desk behaviour

Which backend the Command window's agent runs on, what it is told, and whether the desk looks at images dropped on it. The small print says where a value comes from.

Advanced · Sync to your other machines

Off until you turn it on. Point it at a file in a folder you already sync, or at a profile URL. This section itself never leaves this machine — it holds local paths.
